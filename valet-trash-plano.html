Apartment Valet Trash Service in Plano, TX

MetroMaid Valet provides doorstep trash collection for apartment communities in Plano, Texas. Standard pickup starts at 7 p.m. Sunday through Thursday. Property managers can request a different schedule and get a quote based on occupied unit count, property layout, and pickup frequency.

How doorstep pickup works

Residents

Set bagged household trash outside their doors at the time agreed for their community.

Crew

Collects bagged household trash from residents’ doors on scheduled pickup days.

Property manager

Confirms pickup days, building access, and disposal locations with MetroMaid. Friday and Saturday are off under the standard schedule.
